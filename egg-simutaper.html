Game Overview

Egg SimuTAPer is an idle tapper where you start with a coin crate and build a backyard coop into an automated egg empire.

Studio

GerbilBeard Games

Type

Idle / tapper / chicken-farm sim built to play in the browser.

Status

Play in your browser, and add it to your iPhone Home Screen. No App Store listing yet.

Play Now Privacy Policy

What You Do

  • Tap the crate to earn, then buy supplies, a coop, and chicks
  • Hens lay eggs; sell eggs and automate the farm
  • Feed and water scale with the flock, so restocking matters
  • Use autosave, barn save slots, and file export/import to manage progress
  • Enjoy a portrait, phone-first, 16-bit farm look

How to Play

Start by tapping the crate to earn your first resources. Use those earnings to buy supplies, build a coop, and bring home your first chicks. Once hens start laying eggs, sell the eggs, keep feed and water stocked, and invest in upgrades that automate more of the farm.

Browser Play

Egg SimuTAPer works in Safari and Chrome. On iPhone, open the game in Safari, tap Share, and choose Add to Home Screen for a more app-like experience. Progress saves on the device you play on.
