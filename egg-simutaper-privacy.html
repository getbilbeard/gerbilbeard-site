Overview

This privacy policy applies to Egg SimuTAPer published by GerbilBeard Games.

Guest Play and Local Saves

Egg SimuTAPer supports guest play. Your progress may be stored on the device you use to play through local save data, including autosaves, barn save slots, and exported save files that you choose to keep.

Optional Sign-In

If an optional sign-in feature is present in the game, any information you provide through that sign-in flow is used only to support your access to the game and related features.

Support Requests

If you contact GerbilBeard Games for support, you may provide your name, email address, and the contents of your message. We use that information only to respond to your request and maintain support records as needed.

Sharing

GerbilBeard Games does not sell personal data. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the game, respond to support requests, comply with the law, or protect rights and safety.

Your Choices

You can clear local progress on your device, manage exported save files, and decide whether to use any optional sign-in features that may be available.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.
