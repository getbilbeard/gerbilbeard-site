Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Mexican Train Best Scorekeeper app published by GerbilBeard Games. It explains what information may be involved when you use the app or contact us for support.

Scorekeeping Data

Mexican Train Best Scorekeeper is designed to help you keep track of player names, round scores, totals, and related game information during Mexican Train dominoes play.

Game information you enter is intended for scorekeeping functionality inside the app.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store scoreboards, player names, app preferences, and similar app data locally on your device so your game information is available the next time you open the app.

Support Information

If you contact GerbilBeard Games for support, you may provide information such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message. We use that information only to respond to your request and maintain support records as needed.

Analytics and Diagnostics

This page does not describe Mexican Train Best Scorekeeper as using third-party advertising or third-party tracking. If you choose to share diagnostics, crash reports, or similar information through Apple, GerbilBeard Games may receive limited technical information to help improve stability and performance.

How Information Is Used

  • To provide scorekeeping features inside the app
  • To save app data and preferences on your device
  • To respond to support questions and troubleshoot issues
  • To improve app reliability, performance, and usability
  • To comply with legal obligations or protect rights and safety

Sharing

GerbilBeard Games does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with platform operators or service providers when necessary to distribute the app through Apple, respond to support requests, comply with the law, or protect rights and safety.

Data Retention

Locally stored app data may remain on your device until you remove it, reset the app, or delete the app. Support communications may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to address requests, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations.

Your Choices

You can delete the app and remove locally stored data through your device. You can also choose whether to share diagnostics with Apple and contact GerbilBeard Games with privacy-related questions.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.
